The full release of Casino Blitz is now out! as well as more content and bug fixes!

# NEW CONTENT IN v4.0

The new ability feature can now get a random buff or debuff to make the game harder!
The ability can be used by using the new item Ability Tokens, which can be obtained after playing a game

expect new abilities in the future...

Added +500 money ability
Added -500 money ability

Added Free Bet ability

The free bet ability is a really good buff. After obtaining a free bet the next game you play will auto bet nothing
After this is done however, the game will play like normal
However if you win you double your money
And if you loose you loose nothing!

Added Double Reward ability

The Double Reward ability increases the reward of winning from x2 your bet to x3
which in turn gives you more money

# Balancing

Some of the new abilities where either nerfed, buffed, or scrapped for balancing reasons!

### Scraped abilities (MAY come back in the future)

- Rigged Roll ability
  Was originally going to let you see the result of a single roulette game, however this was obviously could
  been too easy to win with
  This ability will most likely not return in the future
- Shield ability
  Was originally going to let you not loose your money if you lost in a bet, however this could easy make up lots of money at a
  time making playtime really short and would ruin the difficulty standard of the game
  This ability has a chance to get readded to the game in the future

### === Changelog for balancing ===

Double reward was changed from x4 rewards to x3 rewards
Money changing abilities where changed form $600 to $500
